      <h4>Nextick &nbsp;<code>RECENT</code> <code>ACADEMIC</code> <code>PUBLIC</code></h4>
      <sub>Machine learning mini-project that predicts whether a stock's next-day close moves UP or DOWN.</sub>
      <br/><br/>
      <b>What it does:</b> pulls 10+ years of daily data for 36 large-cap US stocks across 5 sectors (Yahoo Finance), engineers 26 time-series features, and compares a majority-class baseline, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest. A Streamlit app sits on top of the pipeline.
      <br/><br/>
      <b>Why it's interesting:</b> the goal was clean evaluation, not a flashy number. Features are built without lookahead leakage, the train/test split is strictly chronological, and the results are reported honestly: the selected Random Forest lands at a test ROC-AUC of about 0.501, basically a coin flip. The project documents why that is a reasonable outcome for daily price direction using basic technical indicators alone.
