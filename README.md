      <br/><br/>
      An MCA mini-project that pulls 10+ years of daily data for 36 large-cap US stocks across 5 sectors (Yahoo Finance), builds 26 features, and compares a majority-class baseline against Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest. A Streamlit app runs on top of the pipeline.

      <b>The honest part:</b> features are built without lookahead leakage and the train/test split is strictly chronological, so the numbers can't be flattered. The selected Random Forest reached a test ROC-AUC of about <b>0.501</b> against a 0.500 baseline. Basically a coin flip, and the project says so. Learning why is the point.
